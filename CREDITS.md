# Credits and origins

ASCII Genesis - Mini is the standalone ESP32 edition of [ASCII Genesis](https://github.com/Mikepyt87/ascii-genesis), created and tested by Mikepyt87 with assistance from OpenAI tools. The first standalone source was separated from original repository commit `93edc82417ec08ebcb6a79afab48ff8e2ff5e8dc`.

**Kirk Baucom** created Asciiquarium. Most upstream ASCII art is credited to **Joan Stark**; **Claudio Matsuoka** expanded its biodiversity. Eleven of the 28 creature/vehicle designs come from that collection. The bundled [upstream source](art/asciiquarium-upstream.pl), notices, and [GPL-2.0-or-later license](art/LICENSE.txt) preserve that foundation. See [Asciiquarium](https://github.com/cmatsuoka/asciiquarium).

Original ASCII Genesis additions include the puffer, bannerfish, spearfish, moray, ray, lanternfish, squid, octopus, turtle, jellyfish, crab, lobster, shark, whale, submarine, lionfish, and peacock mantis shrimp. Their source drawings are in [original-fish.mjs](art/original-fish.mjs). The mirrored colored collection is generated into [fish-art.js](art/fish-art.js).

**xanderchinxyz and the OpenGhost contributors** inspired the early small-display exploration. Their [OpenGhost project](https://github.com/xanderchinxyz/OpenGhost) is credited as inspiration; its code and enclosure are not included here.

**Waveshare** supplies the hardware documentation and JD9853 driver, whose Apache-2.0 notices remain in the driver folder. **Espressif** supplies ESP-IDF, device libraries, and flashing tools. The bitmap font is from **Adafruit GFX**, with its BSD notice in [FONT-LICENSE.txt](preview/FONT-LICENSE.txt).

The Mini-specific renderer, Wi-Fi setup, updater, safe-area layout, and sequential parade are project additions. Credits do not imply endorsement by the original projects.
